# Outreach Playbook

Every message must contain an observation that could only have been written
after reviewing that prospect's website. Do not send follow-ups after an opt-out.

## Public business email or contact form

**Subject:** A small conversion fix for {{PRODUCT}}

Hi {{NAME}} — I found {{PRODUCT}} through {{LAUNCH_SOURCE}} and reviewed the
homepage.

One specific point: {{PERSONALIZED_OBSERVATION}}. A clearer version could be:
"{{MICRO_REWRITE}}"

I offer a 24-hour conversion teardown for newly launched SaaS sites: 10+
prioritized findings, a complete hero rewrite, and a seven-day action plan.
The founding-client price is $49, with a refund if there is no actionable
recommendation.

Details and a sample: {{OFFER_URL}}

If this is not useful, no reply is needed and I will not follow up.

— FirstPass
