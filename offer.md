# FirstPass Offer

## One-sentence promise

Within one business day, turn a newly launched SaaS homepage into a prioritized
conversion action plan that the founder can implement without another meeting.

## Founding-client package — US$49

The customer supplies one public website URL. FirstPass traces the homepage
through its unauthenticated signup handoff without creating an account. The
delivery includes:

1. An executive summary naming the three highest-impact conversion blockers.
2. Ten or more findings, each with page evidence, severity, rationale, and a
   specific recommended change.
3. A complete rewrite of the homepage hero: eyebrow, headline, subheadline,
   primary CTA, and trust line.
4. A desktop and mobile trace from the homepage to the public signup state.
5. A review of message clarity, CTA flow, trust, objection handling, mobile
   usability, basic accessibility, and basic technical performance.
6. Reproducible acceptance checks for each priority change.
7. A seven-day action plan ordered by impact and effort.
8. One clarification round by email within seven days.

Turnaround begins when the URL and payment are received. Scope is one public
marketing website with up to five representative pages plus its unauthenticated
signup handoff; account creation, authenticated product flows, legal compliance
certification, implementation, and analytics setup are not included.

## Honest guarantee

If the delivered report does not contain at least one recommendation the buyer
considers actionable, the buyer may request a full refund within seven days.
This is not a guarantee of increased traffic, conversions, or revenue.

## Why this is a sensible first-sale offer

- A recent launch provides a real timing signal: the founder is actively trying
  to explain and distribute the product.
- The input is only a public URL, so purchasing friction is low.
- The output is concrete and bounded, allowing dependable 24-hour delivery.
- The founding price is low enough for an impulse business purchase while still
  validating willingness to pay.
- A short, free preview can demonstrate judgment without giving away the report.

## Buyer qualification

Prioritize a prospect only if all are true:

- The product launched or materially relaunched within the last 30 days.
- The founder or business provides a public contact route.
- The site has a real product and does not involve illegal, deceptive, adult,
  gambling, predatory lending, or high-risk medical claims.
- At least two meaningful conversion issues are visible without signing in.
- The message can explain those issues specifically and respectfully.

## Fulfillment workflow

1. Confirm the paid order, URL, target customer, and primary desired action.
2. Capture desktop and mobile evidence for the homepage and key pages.
3. Test the five-second value proposition: audience, outcome, differentiation,
   next step, and credibility.
4. Trace the visitor path from landing page to the primary conversion action.
5. Review trust signals, objections, pricing clarity, and decision friction.
6. Check keyboard navigation, headings, labels, contrast, metadata, broken links,
   and basic loading behavior. Do not represent this as formal certification.
7. Draft the hero rewrite and prioritized action plan.
8. Quality-check every claim against visible evidence and deliver as PDF.

- First 3 customers: US$49
- Customers 4–10: US$99

The price increases only after real paid delivery; no fake countdown or scarcity
claim is used.
