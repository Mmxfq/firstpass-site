## 2. Write the plain-language offer sentence

Complete this before writing headlines:

> It is a **[product category]** for **[specific buyer]** that helps them **[complete a recurring job or reach an outcome]** without **[credible old cost, delay, or friction]**.

Evidence check:

- Can the product category be verified from the product?
- Is the buyer named more narrowly than “teams,” “businesses,” or “creators”?
- Is the outcome observable?
- Is the avoided cost or friction supported by the workflow?

If any answer is no, remove or qualify that part.

## 3. Choose one hero job

The first screen should do one primary job. Pick one:

- Explain an unfamiliar category.
- Differentiate inside a familiar category.
- Reduce risk for a sensitive workflow.
- Make a concrete time or cost saving legible.
- Move an already-aware visitor into a trial, demo, or purchase.

## 4. Run the five-second clarity test

Give the current first screen to someone or an agent with no prior context. Hide the page after five seconds. Ask:

1. What is being sold?
2. Who is it for?
3. What gets easier or better?
4. What action should you take next?
5. What fact would you want before trusting it?
